The problem is the GLOBAL threshold: cardiac_surgery_recovery's baseline mortality (~4.7% train+val) is roughly 3-4x lower than the other units (14-19%), so its predicted probabilities sit systematically lower on the same absolute scale, and a single population-wide 90th-percentile cutoff almost never reaches into that unit's score range. Result: the alert fires for barely over 1% of cardiac_surgery_recovery admissions (vs. its 10% population-wide target), catching very few of that unit's deaths, which plausibly reads to the unit director as "inaccurate" / effectively non-functional, even though the underlying risk score ranks that unit's patients about as well as any other.
